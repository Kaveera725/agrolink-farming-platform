Privacy Notice & Data Rights

How AgroLink collects, protects, and handles your personal data in accordance with Sri Lankan law and international privacy standards.

{# 1. Data Collection #}

1 Personal Data We Collect

AgroLink collects only the minimal information strictly necessary to facilitate transparent trade between farmers and wholesale buyers:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, verified email address, role (Farmer or Buyer), and secure salted/hashed passwords.
  • Contact Information: Sri Lankan telephone number and WhatsApp contact for direct farmer-buyer trade communication.
  • Location & Delivery Data: District, delivery address, and geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) for driving distance and delivery fee calculations.
  • Marketplace & Order Activity: Crop listings, produce quantities, harvest notes, order totals, and fulfillment timestamps.
{# 2. Purpose and Lawful Basis #}

2 Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for:

Direct Agricultural Trade

Enabling commercial buyers to discover farmers, negotiate crop harvests, and contact farmers directly via WhatsApp.

Route & Delivery Calculation

Calculating driving routes and distance-tiered delivery fees using Google Maps Routes API without exposing private home coordinates.

{# 3. Your Rights under PDPA 2022 #}

3 Your Data Rights & Self-Service Tools

Under Section 13 and Section 14 of the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA No. 9 of 2022), you hold enforceable rights over your data:

Right to Access & Data Portability

You can request a machine-readable JSON export of your complete profile, listings, and order history at any time.

Right to Erasure & Anonymization

You may request complete account anonymization and deactivation. Once all active orders are settled, your personal identifiers (name, email, phone, location, photo) are permanently purged, and your orders retained in anonymized format for accounting compliance.

{# 4. Contact and Governance #}

Data Protection Governance

AgroLink operates with a strict principle of least privilege. Administrative access is protected by role verification, password policies, and tamper-evident append-only audit logging.
